SELECTED WORK

From raw observations to spatial evidence.

01 / FLAGSHIP

Uplift Geoscience Intelligence Engine

A Virginia Atlantic Coastal Plain pilot integrating geology, USGS NURE geochemistry, mineral occurrences and geographic layers in PostgreSQL/PostGIS. Python, SQL, GeoPandas and GIS pipelines turn disconnected public data into queryable spatial evidence for critical-mineral assessment.

PostGIS · Python · GeoPandas · GIS · Random Forest · Clustering
02 / FEDERAL RESEARCH

Atlantic Coastal Plain Subsurface Integration

USGS work connecting borehole location and depth, core observations, biostratigraphy, wireline logs, legacy records and regional geology to support cross-site correlation, age models and regional subsurface interpretation.

USGS · Boreholes · Stratigraphy · Data QA/QC · GIS
03 / SPATIAL ENVIRONMENTAL ANALYTICS

Arctic & Bering Sea Analytics

Multi-site environmental analysis combining geographic position, water depth, biological assemblages, stable isotopes, environmental variables and time using R, GIS and multivariate statistics.

R · PCoA · Bray-Curtis · Clustering · GIS
04 / GEOAI

GroundwaterGeoAI

An applied earth-intelligence direction integrating hydrogeological observations and geospatial layers to identify patterns, visualize vulnerability and support water-resource interpretation.

Hydrogeology · GeoAI · Spatial Analysis · Decision Support
